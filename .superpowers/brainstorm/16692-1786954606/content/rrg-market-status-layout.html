Market RRG at a glance

The overview answers one question first: what is the current RRG status of each market? Each market uses a fixed daily representative, with BTC and ETH both retained for crypto.

Assumption:“Status” means the representative’s current RRG quadrant plus its latest direction. Crypto becomes “Mixed” when BTC and ETH disagree.
